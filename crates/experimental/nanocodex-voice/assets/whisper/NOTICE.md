Whisper tokenizer and mel-filter assets
=======================================

These files are derived without modification from Apple's `mlx-whisper`
implementation in `ml-explore/mlx-examples`:

- `multilingual.tiktoken` is copied from
  `whisper/mlx_whisper/assets/multilingual.tiktoken`.
- `mel_filters_80.npy` and `mel_filters_128.npy` are the corresponding arrays
  extracted from `whisper/mlx_whisper/assets/mel_filters.npz`.

Source: https://github.com/ml-explore/mlx-examples/tree/main/whisper/mlx_whisper/assets
